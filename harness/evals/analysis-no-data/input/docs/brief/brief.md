# Meeting room booking

Our office has 30 people and 4 meeting rooms. People double-book rooms and
nobody knows which rooms are free. Build a small service where employees can
see room availability for a day and book a room for a time slot. A booking
cannot overlap another booking of the same room. Employees can cancel their
own bookings. Office managers want a weekly report of room usage per room.
Single sign-on exists already; assume the user id arrives in a header.
Budget: 2 days.
